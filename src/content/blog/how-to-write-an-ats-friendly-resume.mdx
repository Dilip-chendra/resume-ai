# How to Write an ATS-Friendly Resume in 2026

Writing a resume is hard enough, but getting it past Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) is even harder. In 2026, over 95% of Fortune 500 companies use an ATS to filter resumes before a human ever sees them.

Here are the best practices for writing an ATS-friendly resume:

## 1. Keep the Format Simple
Avoid complex tables, columns, and graphics. While they might look pretty to a human, ATS parsers often scramble the text inside them, leading to a rejected application.

## 2. Use Standard Fonts
Stick to standard web-safe fonts like Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, or Helvetica. Custom fonts might not render correctly when the ATS tries to extract the text.

## 3. Keyword Optimization
Read the job description carefully and mirror the keywords exactly. If the job asks for "React.js", do not write "React JS" or "ReactJS". The closer you match the job description, the higher your ATS score will be.

## 4. Use Clear Headings
The ATS looks for standard headings to categorize your information. Use traditional headings like:
- Work Experience
- Education
- Skills
- Certifications

Do not get creative and use headings like "Where I've Been" instead of "Work Experience".
